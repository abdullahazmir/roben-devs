Handoff Notes

Slot Conflict

The slot-free check must run again at submit time, not just when the customer first tapped it — two people can both be looking at an open slot. Only one submit may win; the other sees "slot no longer available" immediately, not a generic error.

Technical Error

A failed submit must never hold the slot — no "ghost booking." The customer's entered name and phone number stay filled in so retrying doesn't mean starting over.

Cancellation

Cancelling asks for confirmation once, then releases the slot immediately back to the available pool. The booking record itself is kept (status: cancelled) for the owner's history, not deleted.
